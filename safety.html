Community guidelines

Short and simple. They keep this a kind place to make mistakes in.

Be kind about mistakes

Everyone here is learning. Correct gently, and only when your partner wants it.

Keep it respectful

No harassment, hate or pressure. Disagree politely or leave the conversation.

This is not a dating app

Stop if someone says they are not interested in anything beyond language practice.

Protect your privacy

Do not share your home address or money details. Meet in public places and tell a friend.

Be honest in your profile

Use your real level and your real photo. One account per person.

Speak up

If something feels wrong, report it. You will not be penalised for reporting in good faith.
